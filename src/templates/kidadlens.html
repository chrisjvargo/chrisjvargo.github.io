About KidAdLens

KidAdLens is an academic research application developed and operated by Dr. Chris J. Vargo (Associate Professor of Advertising and Information Analytics, University of Colorado Boulder). The purpose of KidAdLens is to systematically audit and evaluate mobile advertising creatives shown inside child-directed and general-audience mobile applications for age-appropriateness, disclosure practices, and marketing transparency.

This home page is publicly accessible with no account or login required. KidAdLens is a researcher-operated analysis workflow rather than a commercial consumer service, and it does not display advertisements or collect personal information from children or the general public.

What KidAdLens Does

  • Ad Creative Auditing: Processes researcher-collected mobile ad creative screenshots, frames, and metadata to measure content categories, disclosure cues, and child-safety indicators for peer-reviewed scholarly research.
  • Automated Multimodal Evaluation: Runs structured vision-language model inference batches across captured ad creatives to produce reproducible, auditable coding verdicts for academic analysis.
  • Compute Orchestration: Schedules and monitors temporary GPU compute runtimes authorized by the researcher so large-scale evaluations finish reliably within user-defined compute budgets.

How KidAdLens Uses Google Sign-In & Google APIs

KidAdLens uses Google OAuth 2.0 solely to let an authorized researcher connect their own Google Colab subscription to execute automated GPU inference jobs for the KidAdLens research pipeline.

  • Requested OAuth Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/colaboratory (Google Colab).
  • Exact Purpose: Inspecting the authorizing user's Colab subscription tier and available GPU accelerators, provisioning and managing temporary Colab runtimes, transferring ad-evaluation inference batches to those runtimes, retrieving evaluation outputs, and terminating runtimes when work completes.
  • Strict Data Minimization: KidAdLens does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive personal files, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, Google Photos, or any other personal Google Workspace data.
  • No Sale, Sharing, or Model Training: Data accessed via Google APIs is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy: KidAdLens's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
